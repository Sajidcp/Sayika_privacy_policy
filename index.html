Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Sayika ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Sayika to find job opportunities.

Information we collect

Sayika does not ask you to provide personal information and, as described by its operator, does not collect personal information through the app.

Job listings or links may be provided by third-party services or employers. If you follow a link or apply for a job through another service, that service may collect information under its own privacy policy. Please review that service’s policy before sharing information.

Sayika does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising services to collect information. [Confirm this statement before publishing if the app includes third-party SDKs or services.]

How we use information

Sayika provides a way to find job opportunities. We do not use personal information for advertising, profiling, or other purposes.

Sharing and service providers

Sayika does not sell or share personal information. Third-party job sites and services that you choose to visit or use operate independently and have their own privacy practices.

Data retention and security

Because Sayika does not collect personal information through the app, it does not retain personal information. Any information you provide directly to a third-party job site is governed by that service’s policies.

Your choices and rights

Sayika does not maintain personal information submitted through the app. For information submitted to a third-party job site, contact that service directly to exercise your privacy rights.

Children’s privacy

Sayika is a general job-search app and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date above.

Contact us

For privacy questions, contact the Sayika app operator through the support contact provided in the app’s store listing.
